# Banana King 3D: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

Banana King 3D is a game made by Gary Phelps and his son Ashton.

**We do not collect any information.** The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app
purchases, and it never connects to the internet. Everything happens on your device.

- **What we collect:** nothing. No names, emails, locations, device identifiers or usage data.
- **Sharing with others:** there is nothing to share, so nothing is shared with anyone.
- **Storage and deletion:** the game keeps nothing after you close it. Deleting the app removes it completely.
- **Children:** the game is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because it collects no information, there is nothing a
  parent needs to review or delete.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here before the app changes.

**Questions?** Email gary.mphelps@gmail.com or open an issue at
https://github.com/gphelps-dev/banana-king-3d/issues.

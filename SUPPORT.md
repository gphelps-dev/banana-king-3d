## How to play

- **Steer** with ◀ ▶, **run** with ▲, and tap **JUMP**. Tap **JUMP** again in the air to double jump.
- Jump over spikes and lasers, grab the money and gold, and find all 5 axolotls.
- Fell off? You start again at the last stone king statue you passed (it turns gold).
- The laser gate blinks: wait for its lights to turn green, then run through.
- The spinning laser: jump when it comes around, or stand on the stone pillar in the middle.
- Reach the castle and the throne to win.
